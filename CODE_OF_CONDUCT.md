# Code of Conduct

PatchLens AI follows a simple rule: be respectful, constructive, and precise.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Assume good intent while being clear about technical risks.
- Keep feedback focused on code, design, documentation, or behavior.
- Respect privacy and do not share private source code, secrets, or proprietary data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials, tokens, or repository content.
- Repeated off-topic or hostile comments.
- Misrepresenting AI-generated findings as verified facts without evidence.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate these expectations.
